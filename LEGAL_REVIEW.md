# Publication considerations

Reviewed on 27 November 2026. This records the scope and limits of the website update, not a legal opinion or approval to distribute PIH in any jurisdiction.

## What this update does

- Publishes the supplied project philosophy in English and Russian without promises of returns, market value, exchange listing, or financial benefit.
- Distinguishes the one-coin distribution experiment from the existing 1,000,000,000 PIH token supply.
- Displays verified on-chain claim events and balances, with the block used and an explicit unavailable/stale state when a read fails.
- Retains the existing recipient-paid claim mechanism. No issuer payment, email registration, referral, promotional work, or token spending approval is required by the page.
- Explains that Ethereum gas is paid by the recipient, participation may be restricted by applicable law, addresses/transactions are public, and the contract owner can withdraw undistributed PIH.
- Adds no analytics, advertising, lead collection, identity service, new smart contract, or blockchain transaction.

The operator's jurisdiction, legal identity/status, intended audience, and any prior legal opinion have not been supplied. The site does not claim universal eligibility, regulatory approval, or that a disclaimer establishes compliance. No jurisdiction-specific eligibility decision or geographical restriction has been invented.

## Primary sources and limits

### European Union

[MiCA, Regulation (EU) 2023/1114, Article 4](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1114/oj/eng) addresses public offers, including an exemption for certain free offers and limits on that exemption. Personal data, fees, commissions, other benefits, and an intention to seek admission to trading can affect the analysis. Whether the PIH distribution or its existing permissionless trading pool falls within an applicable exemption has **not** been determined. Recipient-paid gas alone does not establish that the complete arrangement is exempt.

### United Kingdom

The [FCA's cryptoasset financial promotion guidance](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/cryptoassets) explains that the regime can apply to firms marketing qualifying cryptoassets to UK consumers, including overseas firms. The site's availability, its wording, and the token's facts must be considered before targeting UK consumers; the project philosophy is not a substitute for a lawful promotion route or an applicable exemption.

### United States

The [SEC's 17 March 2026 interpretation](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/interp/2026/33-11412.pdf) and [announcement](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-30-sec-clarifies-application-federal-securities-laws-crypto-assets) discuss certain airdrops of non-security crypto assets without consideration to the issuer. Those conditions do not themselves classify PIH or approve its distribution. No US securities-law conclusion has been reached. Older SEC staff analysis should not be presented as the current Commission interpretation.

Other jurisdictions, sanctions, financial promotion, consumer protection, privacy, and tax obligations can require a separate assessment based on the operator and intended recipients.

## Required operator follow-up

Identify the operator's country and legal status, intended jurisdictions, token rights and relevant promotion/trading arrangements. Obtain jurisdiction-specific advice where necessary before claiming eligibility or targeting a public distribution campaign. If restrictions are required, implement appropriate enforcement rather than relying only on a checkbox or notice.

GitHub Pages is static hosting. A browser-only country selector or geolocation check can be bypassed. The already-deployed claim contract is permissionless and has not been changed by this update; a website notice cannot prevent direct contract calls.
