The idea behind PIH

A small fraction. A shared idea.

Pihcoin is an independent digital token built around simplicity, accessibility, and freedom of ownership.

One coin can be shared across one million wallets. Each address can receive one millionth of one Pihcoin — 0.000001 PIH.

Pihcoin explores how a single digital coin can reach people across the world through blockchain technology.

The size of a holding does not define belonging. Even the smallest fraction is part of the whole.

No promises of profit. No guaranteed value. A shared digital experiment. Its future depends on people's interest and participation.

  • Transparency

Participation and local rules

Availability depends on applicable law. This page does not establish that participation is permitted in every jurisdiction. Do not participate where prohibited. Pihcoin makes no promise of profit, guaranteed value, or financial benefit.

Wallet addresses and transactions are public on Ethereum. The site uses a public RPC provider and GitHub Pages; those services receive connection information. Language preference and pending transaction details are stored only in your browser.
